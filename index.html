The method

Three fixes, one bearing.

Every instrument and chart in the kit exists to serve one of these three moves. Skip one and the other two drift.

WP.01 — FIX YOUR POSITION

Take stock before you take action.

A blunt audit of where you actually stand — skills, hours, energy, direction — run with the Position Fix worksheet before any goal gets set.

WP.02 — PLOT THE ROUTE

Turn the audit into a course.

Set a 90-day heading with the Career Blueprint, broken into legs short enough that you can actually walk one this week.

WP.03 — HOLD THE BEARING

Small corrections beat big overhauls.

The Weekly Review Log keeps you honest about drift, so you correct course in inches on Friday instead of in miles next year.

Instruments

Tools built to be used, not admired.

Four working instruments. Each does one job well and reports back in numbers you can act on.

INS.01 · GOAL-SETTING

Goal Compass

A single-page instrument that forces one direction, one bearing, one deadline per quarter — no more than that fits on the page, on purpose.

INS.02 · TIME

Time Audit

Log a week in 30-minute legs to see where your hours actually drift, versus where you'd swear they go.

INS.03 · SKILLS

Skill Gap Analyzer

Score your current role against the one you want, instrument by instrument, so the gap is a list and not a feeling.

INS.04 · JUDGMENT

Decision Journal

Log the call, the reasoning, and the outcome. Six months later, hindsight finally has something to teach you.
